# 만약 한강이 5초 동안 사라진다면? — storyboard (vizavi-motion)

Brief: Korean channel 뚝딱 @ttukttak3d, 1080×1920, 30 fps (channel format wins over the skill's 1920×1080/60), ~65 s, captions at 1250 px, no voice-over, music + SFX (Lumean).
Thread: ONE evening picnic, ONE countdown. The 5 story seconds are stretched to ~36 s by slow motion; the millisecond timer is the spine of the video (top HUD, always visible).
Each beat is caused by the previous one: water gone → vacuum → air slams in (wind) → phone flies → hero goes down → water returns with the air inside it → the river erupts → he surfaces with the phone.
Look: blue-hour dusk, sun just set down the river (west), rainbow fountain, warm lamps, teal shadows / warm highlights, DOF on close-ups, motion blur on fast moves.
