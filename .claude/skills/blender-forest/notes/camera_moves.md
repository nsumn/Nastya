# Camera motion analysis (visual estimates from contact sheets + full frames; numbers are approximate)

Conventions: truck = sideways move, dolly = along view axis, pitch = degrees below horizon (+ = looking down, - = looking up). Frame thirds: L/C/R, top/mid/low.

## Shot 1 (f0000-f0105) - top-down ferns/logs
- Move: near-vertical look-down; camera glides FORWARD over the ground (content slides DOWN frame and slightly right). Ground-plane dolly/truck ~0.9-1.2 m total. Last frames start to tilt up: gravel path appears at top-right (horizon creeping in).
- Speed: smooth, roughly constant, perhaps a gentle ease-in; no shake.
- Height: ~2.0-2.5 m start and end (a ~0.6 m fern spans ~25% of frame width). Pitch ~88 deg start -> ~75 deg end (slow tilt-up of ~10-15 deg).
- Lens ~35 mm FF (little distortion, frame covers ~2.3 m wide). DOF: shallow-ish; mid/low ferns and logs sharp, far top edge softer.
- Composition start: fallen log lying upper-left to centre (log centre ~ x 0.3, y 0.3), big fern lower-left (0.35, 0.65), fern cluster lower-right, a second fern at upper-centre. End: log pushed to bottom edge (y ~0.9), mossy rock + fern in centre-right, a second log upper-left, gravel strip top-right corner.
- Light: strong dappled sun, high, from upper-left; dark shade pockets, bright fern patches; high contrast.

## Shot 2 (f0106-f0216) - low ground-level truck
- Move: pure TRUCK LEFT (foreground rocks/ferns slide RIGHT ~45% of frame width; the far dark trunk slides right only ~14%) = parallax, no pan. ~1.0-1.5 m travel. Tiny pedestal-up / tilt drift (path line sinks ~10 px in 640 tile, i.e. horizon goes slightly lower).
- Speed: near constant, perhaps tiny ease at ends. Perfectly smooth, no handheld.
- Height ~0.35-0.5 m (ferns are at eye level), pitch ~0-3 deg (level; horizon/path line at ~48% from top, vanishing back to 25 m of forest).
- Lens ~28-35 mm. Foreground ferns/rocks in near-focus but slightly soft; midground gravel path and tall trunk sharp-ish; distant canopy softer. Mild DOF.
- Composition start: dark tree trunk at x~0.17 (left third), gravel path band across middle (y~0.45-0.5), fern clump centre-low, white fallen log right-mid, sky slivers top. End: trunk x~0.3, rock cluster centre-right low (0.55-0.8, y 0.8), path band wider/left, fern lower-right.
- Light: back/side-lit (sun in front-left, high): trunks dark, foliage glowing, ferns translucent rim light, soft patches on ground (weak-medium).

## Shot 3 (f0217-f0337) - path walk toward the bend
- Move: DOLLY FORWARD ~4-5 m along the path plus slight yaw/truck to the left (trees slide right, path bend drifts right and widens toward the lower right). Slight tilt up of ~5 deg and small height drop.
- Speed: constant walking pace (~0.04 m/frame-ish -> ~1.2 m/s), eased out slightly at the end. Smooth, no shake.
- Height ~1.7 m -> ~1.5 m; pitch ~14 deg down at start -> ~6-8 deg at end.
- Lens ~24-28 mm (wide, strong parallax in ferns). DOF: moderate; path and mid-ground trees sharp, far canopy slightly soft.
- Composition start: path S-curve occupying right half, lower-right diagonal (path enters bottom-centre-right, bends away at top x~0.55), thin white birch left-third (x 0.25), forest bank left-low. End: horizon at ~y 0.33, path enters bottom-centre/left, bends right to x~0.75-0.8, dark trunk at x~0.85, birches left, fern bank low-left.
- Light: sun high (50-60 deg), from camera LEFT-behind/side; heavy dappled shadows on the gravel (strong, ~60-70% contrast), warm bright forest bank.

## Shot 4 (f0338-f0442) - cabin reveal at distance
- Move: slow DOLLY FORWARD ~1 m (cabin A grows ~7%) + TRUCK LEFT ~1.5 m (foreground trees/branches slide RIGHT by ~15-20% of width). Static pitch.

- Height ~1.2 m; pitch ~0-2 deg (level). Distance to cabin A ~14-17 m.
- Lens ~28-35 mm. DOF: cabins and deck crisp; foreground ferns/branches slightly soft; background trees soft.
- Composition start: cabin A gable left (x 0.1-0.43, apex at y 0.05, chimney pipe on top), cabin B right (x 0.5-0.73, apex y 0.2), gravel path in lower-left/centre (y 0.8-1), foreground birch trunk right (x 0.75), deck strip at y ~0.64. End: same but path centred-low (x 0.5), cabin A x 0.08-0.4, cabin B 0.5-0.72, foreground birch at x 0.9, deck y ~0.65-0.7 slightly bigger.
- Light: sun HIGH and from camera-left, slightly behind; tree shadows fall across cabin A gable (strong, patchy), B's left roof slope lit, right face in shade; blue sky with clouds top-centre.

End: cabin A corner window + side wall sliding to x 0-0.62 diagonal, cabin B now x 0.55-0.93 more frontal and smaller, deck corner centre (x 0.47, y 0.85), more gravel in lower right, sky strip top-centre/right.
- Light: sun high, from camera LEFT-behind; cabin B's gable half-lit, soft dappled shadows across cabin A wall; gravel and deck brightly lit; moderate sun-patch contrast; blue sky with clouds.
